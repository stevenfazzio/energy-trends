Each chart names its own source. Data is fetched by this repository, which rebuilds the page every week. Nothing here is an original estimate — it is other people's published numbers, plotted.

Where a chart depends on an accounting convention — primary versus final energy, capacity versus generation, substitution versus direct method — the note under it says which one, because the choice frequently moves the number by more than a factor of two.
